01 · Opening concept

Up in lights.

The new opening turns the dot-matrix wordmark into an LED wall. ZOMZEY’s own line names four kinds of reach, and each one gets an observed brand colour and a scene of its own.

  1. The official wordmark

    The supplied dot-matrix logo, untouched, white on navy. Its upright oval dot is the seed of everything else.

  2. ZOMZEY’s own promise, in colour

    “Promote through people with followers, footfall, fans & audience.” Four lines, sized to fill the column exactly.

    Followers Footfall Fans Audience

  3. LED scenes that spill light

    Duotone photographs resolve out of a 6 × 6.9px oval dot grid and light the page around them.

  4. Two clear starts

    Promote something, or earn from your audience. Both sit above the fold at every width.

  5. Tune in

    Choosing a scene brings it fully into focus, explains an example and lights its word in the headline.
