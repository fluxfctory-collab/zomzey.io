# Design walkthrough

**Concept:** The Living Opportunity Network (working name: ZOMZEY Signal Atlas).

ZOMZEY's value lies in the relationships it makes possible: a project meeting the creator, shop, venue, community or agency that can carry it to an audience. The homepage makes those relationships its organising structure. The dotted wordmark supplies the language: its points become connection endpoints, and its rhythm becomes transit-style routes.

**Opening.** Instead of a headline beside a stock portrait, the first screen is an authored scene. A book launch ticket sits top left, with dotted routes to a reviewer, an independent bookshop and a reading group. The message ("Make the next connection count.") and the two intent actions sit in a reserved centre that no card or line ever crosses. The full stop is drawn as a signal-blue oval, the first node of the network. Four example scenes (Books, Products, Music, Apps) swap people, places and routes without moving the message. Hovering, focusing or tapping a participant lights its route and explains what it could offer.

**Why it suits ZOMZEY.** The scene shows physical and digital reach together, which is what distinguishes the platform from a creator-only marketplace. It starts from the visitor's intent (promoting or earning) rather than from platform taxonomy, while the five participant groups stay one click away in the Explore panel and footer. The identity is preserved: navy, signal blue, role colours as small markers, Instrument Sans, and a slot reserved for the untouched official wordmark.

**The rest of the page** varies its layout while keeping one thread. An editorial relationship story compares online and in-person reach. The same dotted route becomes a five-step process line. A working explorer filters clearly labelled example data and links to the real directory. A navy collaboration record explains agreement, funding, delivery and review, and links to the Protected Payments policy rather than promising outcomes. The closing presents the two starting points as halves of one connection.

**Mobile** is recomposed, not shrunk: headline, description and both actions appear first. The network becomes a vertical story with one project and three participants on a clear line, each expanding on tap.

**Trust.** Nothing on the page is invented proof. There are no totals, testimonials, ratings, verification ticks or client logos. Pricing is qualified, and examples say they are examples.

> *Proposed submission rationale (designer's wording, not a client quotation):* I built the concept around the useful relationships at the heart of ZOMZEY. The dotted identity becomes a network connecting projects with creators, shops, venues and communities. Clear entry choices and familiar navigation keep the experience approachable, while the mobile layout reinterprets the network as a simple vertical journey.
