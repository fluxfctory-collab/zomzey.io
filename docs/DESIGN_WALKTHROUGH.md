# Design walkthrough

**Concept:** Up in lights. (This replaces the earlier opening, "The Living Opportunity Network", at the client's request for a new, more visual idea. The rest of the page is unchanged.)

ZOMZEY's wordmark is drawn from upright oval dots, like an LED sign, and promotion is about being seen. The opening takes both literally: the dotted logo becomes an LED wall, and the wall shows what ZOMZEY actually sells, which is reach.

**Opening.** The headline is ZOMZEY's own line, "Promote through people with followers, footfall, fans & audience." It is set in four balanced lines whose size is calculated from the column, so they never re-wrap from a 320px phone to a 1440px desktop. Each kind of reach takes an observed brand colour: followers orange, footfall yellow, fans green, audience blue. Beside it, four LED scenes use the same colours: a creator at a microphone, a customer in a bookshop, a crowd at a show and a speaker with an audience. Each photograph is a duotone (navy shadows, the colour, a pale tint) that is clear at the centre and dissolves into 6 × 6.9px oval dots towards the edges. Its colour then spills into the page through the same dot grid. On load the wall switches on once, screen by screen.

Choosing a scene brings it fully into focus, dims the others to dots, lights its word in the headline with a dotted underline and explains a hypothetical example, with a link to the matching ZOMZEY page. Two plain actions, promote something or earn from your audience, sit above the fold at every width.

**Why it suits ZOMZEY.** The picture is the promise. Visitors see at once that reach here is not only online followers but also footfall in shops, fans at shows and audiences in communities, which is what separates ZOMZEY from an influencer marketplace. The identity is untouched and amplified: the supplied wordmark, navy, Instrument Sans, and four colours already used on zomzey.io.

**The rest of the page** keeps one thread: dots as connection points. An editorial relationship story compares online and in-person reach. The same dotted route becomes a five-step process line. A working explorer filters clearly labelled example data, a navy record explains agreement, funding, delivery and review, and the closing presents the two starting points.

**Mobile** is recomposed, not shrunk. The headline and both actions fit the first screen. The wall follows as two staggered columns; tablets show one row of four. Every scene works by tap.

**Trust.** Nothing on the page is invented proof: no totals, testimonials, ratings, verification ticks or client logos. Pricing is qualified, and the scenes are labelled "Illustrative scenes".

> *Proposed submission rationale (designer's wording, not a client quotation):* ZOMZEY's dotted logo already looks like a sign in lights, so I made it one. The opening puts ZOMZEY's own promise next to an LED wall of the four kinds of reach it connects, followers, footfall, fans and audience, each in its own colour, while the rest of the page explains how a collaboration actually works.
